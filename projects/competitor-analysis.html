The Setup

Why These Three?

Dot & Key, Foxtale, and WOW Skin Science are three of India's most visible D2C skincare brands — all targeting roughly the same audience (18–34, skincare-aware, digitally native, value-conscious), all investing heavily in Instagram. Their approaches are completely different. So are their results.

I analysed 30 days of Instagram content across six dimensions: content variety, posting frequency, engagement rate, visual identity, community management, and creator strategy. The findings were clearer than I expected.
