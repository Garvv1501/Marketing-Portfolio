Brand Context

Why Noise Coffee?

Noise Coffee is a D2C specialty coffee brand targeting urban millennials and Gen Z who take their coffee seriously — cold brew pouches, single-origin beans, and a strong café aesthetic. Their product is excellent. Their Instagram plays it safe.

The feed is visually beautiful but strategically passive — lots of product shots, not enough story. This 10-day plan is built on 4 pillars: Education (coffee knowledge, brewing methods), Lifestyle (coffee as identity and ritual), Product (showcase + conversion), and Community (UGC, engagement plays). Every post has a job.

The Thinking Behind It

Strategy Notes

Why these posting times?

Indian Instagram audience peaks at 12–2 PM (lunch scroll) and 7–9 PM (post-dinner). Weekends shift earlier — 10 AM to 1 PM. Every post is aligned to the window that matches its content intent.

Why this format mix?

Reels drive reach. Carousels drive saves. Stories drive engagement. Statics maintain product presence. A healthy account uses all four — this plan rotates intentionally so the algorithm sees variety and the audience doesn't get bored.

The saves obsession

Saves are Instagram's highest-intent signal. Days 2, 6, and 9 are built specifically to be saved — cheat sheets, budget lists, and identity content. These compound in reach for days after posting.

Sell vs. value ratio

Only 3 of 10 posts are directly product-led. The other 7 build trust, provide value, or entertain. Audiences who trust you buy from you. Audiences who feel sold to unfollow you.

"Every post should have a job. If it doesn't, it's just noise."
