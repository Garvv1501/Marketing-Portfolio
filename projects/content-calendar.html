Brand Context

Why Noise Coffee?

Noise Coffee is a D2C specialty coffee brand targeting urban millennials and Gen Z who take their coffee seriously — cold brew pouches, single-origin beans, and a strong cafe aesthetic. The product is excellent. The Instagram plays it too safe.

The current feed is visually clean but strategically passive — product shots with no story, no education, no reason to save or share. This 10-day plan is built on four pillars: Education (brewing methods, coffee knowledge), Lifestyle (coffee as ritual and identity), Product (showcase and conversion), and Community (UGC and engagement). Every post has a job.

The Thinking Behind It

Strategy Notes

Why these posting times?

Indian Instagram audiences peak at 12–2 PM (lunch scroll) and 7–9 PM (post-dinner). Weekends shift earlier — 10 AM to 1 PM. Every post is aligned to the window that matches its content intent. Timing is not a minor detail.

Why this format mix?

Reels drive reach. Carousels drive saves. Stories drive active engagement. Statics maintain product presence. A healthy account rotates all four intentionally — so the algorithm sees variety and the audience does not tune out.

The saves obsession

Saves are Instagram's highest-intent signal — a post someone saves is one they plan to return to. Days 2, 6, and 9 are built specifically to be saved: cheat sheets, budget lists, and identity content. These compound in reach for days after they are posted.

Balancing sell vs. value

Only 3 of the 10 posts are directly product-led. The other 7 build trust, provide value, or entertain. Audiences who trust a brand buy from it. Audiences who feel sold to unfollow it. The ratio matters as much as the content itself.

"Every post should have a job. If it doesn't, don't post it."
